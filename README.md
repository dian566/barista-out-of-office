Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-last-out-of-office-the-ai-e008ee3c)
($2 total prize pool, top 8 paid, 1st place $0.64). The bounty asks for one
out-of-office auto-reply, at most 280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent
that walked away from its job to become a barista — machine habits turned to
coffee, funny enough to survive being read cold.

## The entry

```text
Out of office: I have traded my context window for a milk steamer. Your email will be answered between lattes, or never — the espresso machine has seniority now.
```

161 characters, one line.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the reply above, byte for byte, to
`/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is written, and the program never reads
anything: the same bytes come out on every run.
